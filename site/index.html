Built-up extent and heat, 1975–2025

One square is 190,000 people, placed on the cells where those people live, so each city takes the shape of its own built-up area. Each square is coloured by mean daily maximum heat index at that spot, so the hot core and the cooler edges show separately. Every city has its own colour scale, running from its coolest to its hottest square across 1975–2025, so compare colours within a city, not between cities.
